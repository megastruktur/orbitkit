## 3. Investigation Findings & Fix for F1

### Root Cause Analysis (Telemetry-Backed)
Review round 1 identified that after a drag, the first click did nothing and the second opened the menu. Webview telemetry (`09-exec-app.log`) revealed the exact sequence:
1. **Step 3 (Drag Initiation)**:
   - `onpointerdown` at `(148, 148)` sets `pending: true, dragged: false`.
   - `onpointermove` crosses 4px threshold (`dist: 45.6px`), triggering `onDragStart()` -> `start_mascot_drag()`.
   - GTK initiates a native window move drag (`gtk_window_begin_move_drag`), grabbing the X11 pointer.
   - When the drag finishes at `(700, 400)`, mouse release is consumed by the native GTK grab. WebKitGTK never receives a `ButtonRelease` event, leaving WebKitGTK's internal button state stuck down, and never emits a DOM `click` event.
2. **Step 4 (First Click after Drag)**:
   - `xdotool click 1` sends X11 `ButtonPress` followed by `ButtonRelease`.
   - Because WebKitGTK believed Button 1 was already down, it ignored `ButtonPress` (no DOM `pointerdown` event was dispatched).
   - Upon receiving `ButtonRelease`, WebKitGTK dispatched DOM `pointerup` and DOM `click`.
   - Without a timer, `dragged` remained `true` (waiting for a terminating click that never came, while `pointerdown` was missed). `onclick` swallowed that first click as post-drag suppression.
3. **Step 5 (Second Click)**:
   - WebKitGTK now knew Button 1 was up, and `dragged` had finally been reset to `false`.
   - A normal `pointerdown` -> `pointerup` -> `click` sequence occurred, opening the menu one click too late.

### Fix
In `createDragGesture.ts`:
- Added `dragClearDelay` (default 400 ms).
- When a drag begins (`dist > threshold`), a timer is started to automatically clear `dragged = false` (superseded in round 2: timer is armed on drag-end signals rather than drag start — see §4).
- By the time the user or test script clicks the mascot at its new position in Step 4 (~1.5s later), `dragged` has already cleared.
- Even though WebKitGTK dispatches only `pointerup` and `click`, `onclick` sees `dragged: false` and immediately calls `onToggle()`.
- The radial menu opens on the very first click in Step 4, and closes on the second click in Step 5.
